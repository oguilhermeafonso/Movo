# Movo | Personal Finance Manager

Simplifying financial planning, one month at a time.

### About the Project

Movo is a personal finance manager developed in C, designed to make monthly financial planning simpler, more intuitive, and accessible.
The project was born from a common problem: managing personal finances can become unnecessarily complicated. People often struggle to keep track of their income, expenses, and upcoming bills, especially when unexpected changes affect their financial plans.
Life doesn't always go according to plan, and managing money shouldn't make it harder.

### The Problem

Many financial management tools can be complex, time-consuming, or impractical for everyday use.
People need a simple way to understand their financial situation, organize upcoming payments, and adapt their plans whenever something changes.
The challenge isn't just tracking money. It's making financial organization practical enough to become part of everyday life.

### Our Solution

Movo aims to provide a straightforward way to organize and visualize personal finances through a monthly overview.
The idea is to help users:
Track their monthly income and expenses.
Organize upcoming bills and expected payments.
Visualize their financial situation in one place.
Keep track of financial transactions.
Adjust their planning as their financial situation changes.
Instead of overwhelming users with unnecessary features, Movo focuses on what matters most: clarity, simplicity, and practicality.
